SharpaDex · clean_plate_with_eraser · MuJoCo replay

The same commands, replayed on a simulated Sharpa robot

Four recorded episodes. For each, three command streams drive a simulated D01 robot through a scene rebuilt from the recording's cameras: the operator's commands, the commands of the model with force, and the commands of the model without force. The models predicted their commands from the real recorded cameras, joints and forces; they never see the simulation.

Use this for the eraser, not the plate. The eraser is picked up in all 12 runs. The plate is lifted by the operator's own commands in only 2 of 4 episodes, so a model failing on the plate says nothing about the model.

Outcome of every run

Highest point each object reaches above where it started. An object counts as lifted above cm.

What this simulator can and cannot show

  • It replays commands; it is not closed-loop. A model's commands were computed from real observations, so the simulation shows whether those commands are physically plausible, not whether the model could finish the task on its own.
  • The hands are pulled to their commanded poses and the arms follow passively. The public arm model does not reproduce the dataset's arm joint angles (3 to 5 cm off at the hand), so the arms are not driven joint by joint. The hands lag their commanded pose by about 17 mm on average.
  • The plate and eraser are simple shapes sized from the cameras and from where the fingers first close. Pinching a thin plate off a table is sensitive to that shape, which is why it fails with the operator's own commands in half the episodes.
  • Simulated fingertip forces run 1.2 to 2 times the real ones in three of the four episodes. Compare their timing with the real trace, not their size.
  • Wiping is counted when the eraser presses on the plate with more than 1 N. It depends on the plate being held, so it is only meaningful in runs where the plate was lifted.
