How to read a video

recordingpolicy head left wrist right wrist
  • The world model: PrimeSim (Wan 2.2 TI2V 5B), fine-tuned on the three tasks. It generates the head and both wrist views from joint commands.
  • Start: one real frame, 3.2 s into a held-out recording. From there the right column is generated.
  • Loop: every 1.5 s the policy sees the latest generated frames and is called three times, 0.5 s apart. Its commands drive the video model.
  • Force inputs: fingertip force and joint torque are replayed from the recording, because the video model produces pictures only. They match the generated scene only while the rollout stays close to the recording.
  • Numbers under a video: distance between the policy's commands and the operator's at the same time, over all joints, one value per 3.2 s. It measures divergence, not error: after a few seconds the two need not be doing the same thing.
  • Not measured: task success. Nothing checks whether the plate is wiped or the plug is in.

Videos play at the generated rate of 10 frames per second. The same episodes are used for every seed, so the left columns of a row are identical. Files and per-episode numbers: reports/world_model_primesim, table: world_model_policy.md.
